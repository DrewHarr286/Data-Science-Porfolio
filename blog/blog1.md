Responsibilities of Using Human Data When you're dealing with data about real people, you have to be really careful. It's not just numbers on a screen; it's people's lives. Privacy: Nobody wants their personal stuff leaked or sold to random companies.  I think anonymizing data isn't always enough because people can still be tracked down if you're smart enough.Fairness and Bias: Models learn from past data, and past data is full of unfairness. I think if a computer model is deciding who gets a job or a loan it can easily repeat those same old things, if nobody checks it.Consent: People deserve to actually know how their data is being used, instead of agreeing to a 50-page terms of service agreement that literally no one reads.Not using data against people. Just because you collected data for one good reason doesn't mean you should hand it over to be used for aggressive ads or spying on people.
